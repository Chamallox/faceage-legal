About FaceAge

FaceAge is a playful, for-fun iOS app that takes a selfie and uses AI to generate a guess at how old different parts of your face look. It is not a medical tool. It does not measure your real skin age, does not diagnose any condition, and does not provide personalized skincare or health advice. Any tips shown inside the app are general cosmetic information only and are clearly labelled as such.

Need Help?

Contact us — For any questions, issues, or feedback, email us at support@faceage.app. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Manage subscriptions — To cancel or change your subscription, go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

Delete your data — Open FaceAge, go to Settings, and tap "Delete Account". All your data will be permanently removed. You can also uninstall the app to delete all local data.
