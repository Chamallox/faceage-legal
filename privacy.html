Privacy Policy

Last updated April 22, 2026

This policy explains how FaceAge handles your information. FaceAge is an entertainment app; it is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice.

What we handle

  • Your selfie. Sent to a third-party AI provider only to generate your for-fun result, then discarded. We do not keep a copy.
  • Optional preferences you enter in the app. Stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase and subscription status, handled by Apple.
  • Anonymous, aggregated usage data used only to improve stability and performance. It does not identify you.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not build an advertising profile of you.
  • We do not store your photos on our servers.
  • We do not require you to create an account or hand over your email.

Third parties

We use a small number of service providers to run the app (for example, an AI provider that generates your result, and payment/subscription handling through Apple). These providers receive only what is strictly needed to perform their function and are bound by their own privacy terms.

Storage

Your scan history and preferences live on your device. Uninstalling the app or using "Delete Account" in Settings removes them.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete Account, or uninstall the app.
  • Opt out of anonymous analytics: decline tracking when iOS asks.

Children

FaceAge is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above reflects the latest version.
