Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

FaceAge - Skin Analysis ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Photos: When you take a selfie for analysis, the photo is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. We do not store your photos on our servers. Photos remain on your device and are only transmitted for the duration of the analysis.

Profile Information: Your gender, age, and skin concerns are collected during onboarding to personalize your analysis. This data is stored locally on your device.

Usage Data: We may collect anonymous analytics data (screen views, feature usage) to improve the app experience. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Purchase Data: Subscription and purchase information is managed by Apple and RevenueCat. We do not have access to your payment details.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered face analysis using your selfie and profile information
  • To generate personalized skincare recommendations
  • To track your progress over time (stored locally on your device)
  • To improve app performance and user experience through anonymous analytics

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI: Processes your selfie to generate face analysis results. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Manages subscriptions and purchases. Subject to RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase (Google): Provides analytics and data storage. Subject to Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your scan history and profile data are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage. We do not maintain a central database of user photos or personal information. When you delete the app, all local data is removed.

5. Your Rights

  • Delete your data: You can delete all your data from within the app (Settings → Delete Account) or by uninstalling the app.
  • Opt out of analytics: You can decline analytics tracking when prompted by iOS.
  • Access your data: All your data is visible within the app. There is no hidden data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

FaceAge is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. AI Disclaimer

FaceAge uses artificial intelligence to analyze facial features. Results are for entertainment and informational purposes only. The app is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Consult a dermatologist for personalized treatment recommendations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@faceage.app.
