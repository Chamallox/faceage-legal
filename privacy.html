Privacy Policy

Last updated April 4, 2026

FaceAge respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Photos — When you take a selfie for analysis, the photo is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. We do not store your photos on our servers. Photos remain on your device and are only transmitted for the duration of the analysis.

Profile Information — Your gender, age, and skin concerns are collected during onboarding to personalize your analysis. This data is stored locally on your device.

Usage Data — We may collect anonymous analytics data to improve the app. This data cannot identify you personally.

Purchases — Subscription and purchase information is managed entirely by Apple and RevenueCat. We never see your payment details.

How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered face analysis using your selfie and profile
  • To generate personalized skincare recommendations
  • To track your progress over time (stored locally)
  • To improve app performance through anonymous analytics

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to power the app:

  • OpenAI — Processes your selfie to generate analysis. Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Manages subscriptions. Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — Hosts our secure API proxy. Privacy Policy
  • Upstash — Temporary rate-limit storage. Privacy Policy

Data Storage & Security

Your scan history and profile data are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage. We do not maintain a central database of user photos or personal information. When you delete the app, all local data is removed.

Your Rights

Delete your data — Settings → Delete Account, or uninstall the app.

Opt out of analytics — Decline tracking when prompted by iOS.

Access your data — All data is visible within the app.

Children's Privacy

FaceAge is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

FaceAge uses artificial intelligence to analyze facial features. Results are for entertainment and informational purposes only. This is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the date above.
